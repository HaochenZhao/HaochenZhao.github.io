Haochen Zhao 赵皓晨

I received my master's degree in computing, specializing in artificial intelligence, from the National University of Singapore. Previously, I studied artificial intelligence at Yuanpei College, Peking University. My research interests span language models, efficient AI, and reliable agents.

I am interested in how models learn to reason, use computation effectively, and behave reliably in open-ended environments. My work explores post-training and reasoning, efficient inference, and evidence grounding and safety for language models and agents.
